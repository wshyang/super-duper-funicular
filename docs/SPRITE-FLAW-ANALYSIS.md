## Measurement method and limits

Centroid-aligned silhouette IoU uses alpha ≥128 and a 0.78 near-duplicate threshold. Alignment translates the second mask by the rounded centroid offset; it does not scale or otherwise normalize the image. Height and baseline ranges are measured from the keyed alpha bounds within each color row. The spill diagnostic counts opaque strong-magenta pixels (R>205, B>195, G<75, with R−G>120 and B−G>115) and magenta-hued pixels (R/B>110, G<155, both excesses>40, |R−B|<80) still touching the cleared matte in an 8-neighbourhood. Edge cleanup expands through at most eight such layers and stops at non-fringe pixels, typically the dark outline; it is a heuristic, not semantic segmentation. The lower-band cluster/separation proxy uses opaque pixels in the lowest 16% of each registered cell; it is not semantic foot or limb tracking. These are source-sheet diagnostics, not evidence of clinical biomechanics or general gait realism.
